## Recommendations

### Priority 1: Finalize business essentials
1. Replace placeholder contact details with the actual firm information.
2. Confirm brand wording, tone, and legal business name.
3. Add a final set of authentic service descriptions and benefits.

### Priority 2: Improve conversion
1. Connect the contact form to an email service or backend endpoint.
2. Add a stronger call-to-action flow such as booking, consultation, or quote request.
3. Include trust signals like years in business, certifications, number of clients, and service guarantees.

### Priority 3: Strengthen content quality
1. Add client testimonials and case studies.
2. Include a FAQ section to answer common client concerns.
3. Clarify exactly what the firm offers to different client types (small business, freelancers, startups, etc.).

### Priority 4: Improve technical quality
1. Move page-specific styles into the main stylesheet for consistency.
2. Add page-specific SEO metadata and titles for better search engine visibility.
3. Optimize performance and mobile responsiveness before final launch.

### Priority 5: Prepare for launch
1. Add privacy policy, terms, and legal pages.
2. Test all pages across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
3. Validate broken links, images, and form behavior before publishing.
4. Set up analytics and conversion tracking after launch.
