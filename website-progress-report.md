## Missing / Pending Work

### Content and credibility gaps
- [ ] Real company address, phone number, and email should be updated
- [ ] Business name, legal branding, and tone need final approval
- [ ] Testimonials or client success stories are missing
- [ ] Case studies or sample work are not yet visible
- [ ] No formal FAQs section for common questions

### Conversion and lead generation gaps
- [ ] Contact form does not send data to email or backend service yet
- [ ] No booking or consultation workflow is implemented
- [ ] No clear lead capture strategy beyond WhatsApp and basic form
- [ ] No trust badges, certifications, or compliance mentions yet

### UX and polish gaps
- [ ] Some pages still feel more like mockups than fully finished marketing pages
- [ ] Visual consistency can be improved across all sections and pages
- [ ] More spacing refinements and content hierarchy improvements are still needed
- [ ] Services page styling appears partly inline rather than fully centralized in the main stylesheet

### Technical gaps
- [ ] No SEO meta descriptions or keywords for each page
- [ ] No social sharing tags or Open Graph metadata
- [ ] No analytics tracking installed
- [ ] No real backend or database integration
- [ ] No form validation success/error states beyond the simple message

### Business readiness items
- [ ] Privacy policy, terms, and legal pages are still missing
- [ ] No appointment scheduling or CRM connection
- [ ] No portfolio of tax, bookkeeping, or advisory deliverables
- [ ] No clear differentiation from other accounting firms yet

## Recommendations

### Priority 1: Finalize business essentials
1. Replace placeholder contact details with the actual firm information.
2. Confirm brand wording, tone, and legal business name.
3. Add a final set of authentic service descriptions and benefits.

### Priority 2: Improve conversion
1. Connect the contact form to an email service or backend endpoint.
2. Add a stronger call-to-action flow such as booking, consultation, or quote request.
3. Include trust signals like years in business, certifications, number of clients, and service guarantees.

### Priority 3: Strengthen content quality
1. Add client testimonials and case studies.
2. Include a FAQ section to answer common client concerns.
3. Clarify exactly what the firm offers to different client types (small business, freelancers, startups, etc.).

### Priority 4: Improve technical quality
1. Move page-specific styles into the main stylesheet for consistency.
2. Add page-specific SEO metadata and titles for better search engine visibility.
3. Optimize performance and mobile responsiveness before final launch.

### Priority 5: Prepare for launch
1. Add privacy policy, terms, and legal pages.
2. Test all pages across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
3. Validate broken links, images, and form behavior before publishing.
4. Set up analytics and conversion tracking after launch.

## Summary

This website already demonstrates good visual structure, pricing/service positioning, and a polished modern brand direction. The strongest progress is in the design foundation and page architecture. The main remaining work is in converting the mockup into a fully credible business website with complete content, working contact lead generation, and final launch polish.

Overall evaluation: Good foundation, strong visual direction, ready for final content and conversion improvements.
